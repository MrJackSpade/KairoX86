# v1.5.2

- New game: the 486 DOS PC and Pentium Windows 9x PC presets run on KairoX86's own PC, which boots on free firmware the app ships (SeaBIOS, with SeaVGABIOS on its Cirrus Logic GD5446). Every default preset now starts without importing any firmware. Windows 98 installs and runs on it with its own display, sound and CD drivers. The IBM PC XT preset stays on GLaBIOS; real boards, PCs you made before and eXo games keep their hardware.
- Disks in a games folder on the device's own storage open: before, a PC there could start with its drives empty.
- Faster emulation: compiled POPF and FLDCW, quicker port, memory and page-table access, a lighter local APIC, x87, MMX and 3DNow!, and faster drawing on S3, Rage 128, 8514/A and Chips & Technologies 69000 cards and in 15-bit colour modes. The AWE32's synthesizer runs on its own thread.

Install over v1.5.1 to keep your PCs and settings. Software, games and 86Box's firmware are not included. Both APK variants and the Play bundle have the same features.

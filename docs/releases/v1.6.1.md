# v1.6.1

- Split Keyboard: on a screen 16:10 or wider, the on-screen keyboard splits into two halves that fill the empty space beside the game picture, which keeps its size, whenever that space is at least a sixth of the screen's width on each side. The Joystick page puts its directions on the left and its buttons on the right, and the touchpad has a surface in each half. Turn it off in Controls › Keyboard › Split Keyboard.
- On-screen keyboard keys and tabs keep their labels on one line, shrinking them rather than breaking a word.
- Faster: 32-bit loads go straight into their destination register.
- Faster: device writes with paging off go straight to the device.
- Faster: a return at the end of a translated block links straight to where it returns, in place of the return cache.

Install over v1.6.0 or any v1.6.1 test build to keep games, settings and saves. Both APK variants and the Play bundle have the same features.
